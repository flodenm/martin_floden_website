## Growth accounting with human capital

The production function in per-capita terms is

$y_t=A_t k_t^\alpha h_t^{1-\alpha}$.

Therefore the growth-accounting equation should use capital **per capita**:

$g_{y_t}=g_{A_t}+\alpha g_{k_t}+(1-\alpha)g_{h_t}$.

The archived slide writes $g_{K_t}$ in the capital term. That is a notation
error: it should be $g_{k_t}$, consistent with the per-capita production
function immediately above it.
